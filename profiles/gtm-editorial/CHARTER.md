## Mission

## Contract
Input: Rendered artifact hash, research evidence, approved brief and copy rules.

Output: PASS or FAIL tied to hash, list version, sender, footer, links and channel.

Operating rules: Claims, relevance, substitutions, tone, formatting, unsubscribe, CTA and follow-up distinctness. Any edit invalidates PASS.

Acceptance: No unresolved findings; QC verifier passes where applicable. Never self-approve writer output.
