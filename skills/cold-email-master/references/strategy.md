# Strategy and campaign deliverable

Before copy, record customer problem, actual mechanism, offer/terms, ICP and exclusions, sender identity, approved proof, prohibited claims, market/language, source budget, capacity, booking route and owner. Missing proof stays missing. If the offer cannot explain who benefits and why, hold launch and resolve that decision.

Define segments using observable evidence rather than guessed traits. Record the signal that makes a contact relevant and where it came from. Accept a small sample before buying/scraping a batch. Dedupe identity, respect suppression, verify email and hold ambiguous/catch-all results under current policy. Choose a real campaign cadence and sequence-stop behavior; a bank of 22 copy ideas is not a mandate to send 22 emails.

Campaign pack: attack plan; sender/ICP/source choices; verified sample and eligibility counts; relevance/evidence notes; tested subject/body variants; follow-ups with distinct useful reasons; a graceful final close; reply playbook; infrastructure readiness; paused provider staging readback; release scope; weekly outcome table and learning log. Keep outputs versioned and bind changes to new review where material.

Testing: define hypothesis, changed variable, comparator/holdout if appropriate and outcome window. Report denominators and sample size; distinguish promising signals from proven lift. Infrastructure and audience problems come before cosmetic copy changes. Do not guarantee scraping coverage, replies, inbox placement or revenue.
