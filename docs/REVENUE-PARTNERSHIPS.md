# Revenue Partnerships Program Manager

**Contextual relationships for a complete partner program.** Affiliate, referral and other revenue partnerships share the same foundation: understand the person, remember the conversation, track commitments and make the next message relevant.

The demonstration uses fictional sample contacts. It shows contextual cards, introductions, preferences, commitments, personalized drafts and recorded outcomes. It does not establish James’s live connections.

## What this role does

Discover and qualify partners, prepare onboarding, support activation, track introductions, maintain contextual relationship cards, draft personalized follow-up and review attribution against authoritative records. Program terms and payouts remain owner decisions. Reuse the existing graph and sender ownership.

In James’s workforce this role is an optional addition. Conversion Specialist owns customer conversations; the cold-email team owns campaign execution. Partner operations are separately enabled with `--affiliate`; stable profile and source identifiers remain `affiliate-manager`.
