# Shared email-team contract

Status: staged design; no runtime profiles or jobs were installed by this pack.
The user's October 8 request selects separate role/avatar profiles instead of
one specialist identity. These 16 roles are the email-focused subset, not the
archive's complete 22-profile migration.

Each profile has its own charter, memory, sessions and narrowly assigned skills.
They may share an Orgo computer; serialize shared browser actions. A profile is
not an OS security boundary. Verify the installed Hermes version before generating
config or creating runtime profiles. Give credentials only to the relevant adapter.

Overall Email Campaign Manager routes work and tracks readiness. Campaign owners
own their offers, ICP, proof, calendar and results; they request shared specialists
rather than recreating their pipelines. Data uses Databox's service or existing
agent with traceable jobs and budgets. List Manager owns identity, eligibility,
verification records and suppression. ESP owns verification policy and health.

Flow: campaign brief → Data → List Manager/verification → Writer → independent
Editorial → ESP clearance → Instantly or SMTP staging/readback → applicable release
→ execution/replies → Attio updates and ClickUp operating progress.

Editorial reviews the exact rendered version independently of Writer: claims,
personalization, tone, subject, signature, links, unsubscribe/footer, variable
substitution, plain text and HTML where applicable. Changes invalidate its PASS.
Editorial gives clearance; the platform operator executes and confirms send state.

Instantly is the sole Instantly executor, including reply sends requested by Reply
Agent. SMTP owns Listmonk/SMTP execution; confirm its monitored reply inbox and
appropriate mailbox executor. Attio is the sole CRM writer; ClickUp is the sole
operating-task writer. Suppress and stop sequences immediately on unsubscribe or
reply, without waiting for CRM. No profile bypasses these owners.

Use durable task/event IDs and versioned artifacts, with per-destination receipts,
readbacks and dedupe. Ambiguous writes require reconciliation before retry.
ClickUp tracks projects, decisions, owners, blockers and won-client work; prospects
live in Attio. Missing sync is visible queued work, never claimed complete.

Keep contact data, credentials, browser cookies, runtime databases and raw backups
outside Git. Curate dated memory rather than importing whole old chats. Historical
IDs, capacity, health and permissions require current evidence. No fabricated
proof, consent, identity, completed send or booking. Preserve the source prohibition
on “no upfront cost.” Unknown verification and health remain unresolved; catch-alls
are HOLD unless a campaign's current explicitly adopted policy allows them.

Draft/stage only during migration. Jobs begin disabled. Preserve existing pauses
and campaign-specific release rules; no paid plan is approved on the user's behalf.
Reuse applicable standing budgets and authorizations without widening them.
Independent Editorial is required; Writer's self-review cannot replace it.

Wake profiles for assigned work, actual events or due work; avoid idle model polling.
One shared health snapshot feeds campaign owners. First validate replay and shadow
results, then prepare a reviewed rollout with restore evidence and no overlapping
old writers or triggers. Avatar presentation is configured at runtime separately
from the charter; no new avatar art is included here.
