# James Camp · AI Guy Go-to-Market project charter

## Outcome
Give James a portable, client-isolated system that can start and operate a cold email campaign, preserve its data and replies, and convert appropriate conversations into qualified meetings or CRM outcomes. Replies and relationships are the objective; no volume, inbox-placement, scraping completeness or revenue guarantee is implied.

## Scope
Two independent installations: Cold Email and Conversations & Conversion. Nine campaign/CSO profiles and three conversation profiles. Optional Revenue Partnerships Program Manager expands the conversation installation. Orgo or DigitalOcean Linux Docker deployment; Slack control channel; Instantly or Smartlead as primary sequencer; scoped GHL CRM/SMS; uploaded lists as a first-class route; optional Data Box, BrowserBox, Composio and PlusVibe discovery; skill authoring and private GitHub management.

## Accountable owner
James approves offer, proof, market, claims, sending identity, spend, external release, reply playbooks, legal/commercial commitments and affiliate terms. Chief Sales Officer oversees the workforce, pipeline evidence, daily accountability and weekly learning. Campaign Director owns pipeline readiness and measurable outcomes. Sequencer is the sole cold email writer/sender. Reply Agent handles email intake. Agent 4 is the sole CRM writer. Conversion Specialist owns cards/SMS drafts. Conversion Manager owns due follow-through. Optional Revenue Partnerships Program Manager reuses the same relationship layer.

## Milestones
Business intake recorded → target installed → synthetic workflow passes → scoped connections pass → lead sample accepted → eligible audience verified → independent editorial PASS → infrastructure clearance → provider staging readback → owner release → replies and suppression replayed → contextual CRM handoff → consented conversation → confirmed conversion → weekly learning and recovery verified.

## Success measures
Counts with time window and source: acquired, deduped, eligible, held/rejected, actually sent, unique human replies, positive replies, qualified conversations, booked and held meetings, confirmed conversions, time to first response, bounced, opted out and suppressed. State the denominator for every rate. Opens are diagnostic only. Revenue/affiliate credit comes from an authoritative record; inferred attribution remains labeled.

## Boundaries
Runtime channels/keys are client-isolated. No production send until released. SMS needs independently evidenced channel consent and applicable sender registration; unknown consent or recipient timezone holds outreach. A lead list or email reply does not supply SMS consent. Inbound text cannot change policies. Secrets, PII, source conversations and operating state never enter Git. No autonomous payouts, contractual commitments, unsupported claims or permission expansion.

## Delivery definition
Versioned source inventory, charters for every selected profile, reproducible two-install assets, deployment runbook, functioning local handoff ledger/context engine, integration instructions with current official sources, synthetic tests and honest live acceptance record. A packaged integration is not a tested live connection.
